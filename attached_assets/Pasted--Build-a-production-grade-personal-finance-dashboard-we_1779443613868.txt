# IMPORTANT FINANCIAL CONCEPTS TO VISUALLY TEACH

The app should naturally educate the user about:

## Cash Flow

Money in vs money out.

## Budgeting

A plan for money.

## Assets vs Liabilities

Assets make money.
Liabilities consume money.

## Net Worth

Assets - Liabilities.

## Emergency Fund

Safety buffer.

## Interest

Cost of borrowing or earning.

## Compound Interest

Interest earning interest.

## Return on Investment

Profit from investments.

## Inflation

Money losing value over time.

## Investing

Growing money long term.

These concepts should appear as:

* Smart tooltips
